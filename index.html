              Data freshness, safety & privacy

              Emergency? Dial 112. This app is not an emergency service.

              Disclaimer

              Siliguri Puja Navigator is an independent community project. It is not run, endorsed or approved by the police, the municipal corporation or any puja committee. Information may be incomplete, late or wrong. Always follow signs and the directions of traffic police on the ground. Nothing here shows live traffic or road conditions.

              Privacy

              No analytics, no tracking, no accounts. Your location is requested only when you press a locate button; it stays in memory on your device, is never stored and never sent anywhere by this app. The map is drawn from OpenStreetMap tile servers, which receive your IP address and the map area you view. If you tap a navigation link, Google Maps opens and receives the destination. The app saves one timestamp in your browser (when data last refreshed) and caches its own files so it can load offline.
