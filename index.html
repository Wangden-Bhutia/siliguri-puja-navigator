                Data freshness

                What the labels mean

                • Official – taken from an official order or notice.
                • Admin-verified – checked by the maintainers against a named source.
                • Unconfirmed / Unverified – not checked. Do not rely on it.
                • Demo – sample placeholder, not real.
                • Active / Upcoming / Expired – whether a published schedule includes the time being checked. It is not proof about the road right now.

                Disclaimer

                Siliguri Puja Guide gathers published visitor and traffic information in one place. Information may be incomplete, late or wrong, and it can change on the ground at any time. This app does not control traffic and does not show live road conditions. Always follow on-ground directions from traffic police.

                Privacy policy

                No analytics, no tracking, no accounts. Your location is requested only when you tap a button for it; it stays in memory on your device and is never stored or sent by this app. The map is drawn from OpenStreetMap tile servers, which receive your IP address and the map area you view. If you tap a directions link, Google Maps opens and receives the destination. The app saves in your browser one timestamp (when data last refreshed), your day/night choice, and its own files so it can work offline.
